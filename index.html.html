Sync your Google Sheet

Load the latest vocabulary from your published Google Sheet. When you add words to the sheet, open this app and click “Sync Google Sheet” again. This imports words into this browser; progress you mark in the app stays local.

Your sheet is published to the web, so anyone with its published link may be able to read the vocabulary. This connection is read-only: changes made in the app do not write back to the sheet.

Or import a CSV file

Choose a CSV file exported from Excel or Google Sheets. The first row should contain column names.

✨ Add a word prepared by ChatGPT

Ask ChatGPT to research a new word, review the details, and approve it. Then copy the tab-separated row ChatGPT gives you and paste it below. The app will add it to your local vocabulary.

Field order: German → English → Category → Article/full form → Example → Translation → Your notes → Pronunciation → Importance → For exam (Yes/No) → Azeez suggestion (Yes/No). You can paste multiple rows, one word per line.

Export / backup

Export all words and your progress as a CSV backup. Keep a copy somewhere safe.

This app does not send your data anywhere. Clearing browser data can erase locally saved entries, so keep backups.
